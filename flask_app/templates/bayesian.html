How to read this model

What the model estimates

A Bayesian hierarchical linear regression:

inflation(t) = α_country + β × conflict(t-1) + ε
  • α, country specific baseline inflation
  • β, effect of lagged conflict on inflation
  • ε, unexplained noise

Key finding. β = {{ "%.3f"|format(model.beta_mean) }}, 94% HDI [{{ "%.2f"|format(model.hdi_low) }}, {{ "%.2f"|format(model.hdi_high) }}]. The interval contains zero, so the data do not establish a positive or negative effect.

Why the result is uncertain
LimitationConsequence
N = 12 observationsLow statistical power
Annual national CPIToo aggregated
No confoundersRainfall, oil prices uncontrolled
Linear model onlyMisses threshold effects

P(β > 0) = {{ "%.1f"|format(model.prob_pos) }}%, conflict may raise inflation.
P(β < 0) = {{ "%.1f"|format(model.prob_neg) }}%, conflict may lower it. Neither is conclusive. Sub national monthly price data would substantially improve the model.

Model specification and sampling 
μ_α ~ Normal(3, 5)
σ_α ~ HalfNormal(3)
α_c ~ Normal(μ_α, σ_α)     # country specific intercept
β   ~ Normal(0, 2)         # effect shared across countries
σ   ~ HalfNormal(5)
inflation ~ Normal(α_c + β × conflict_z, σ)

Sampling. NUTS, 4,000 draws over 4 chains, 16,000 posterior samples, 2,000 tuning steps, target_accept 0.95, all R hat at 1.0.
Software. PyMC v5, ArviZ, Python 3.10.
